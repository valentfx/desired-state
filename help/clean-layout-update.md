## 2026-10-03 — saved signal review and H10 overview
Review session is available after stop and from a stopped session in Analyze/History (also reached through Participants -> View sessions). The new Session analysis page keeps one selected session-time range for HR, HRV, EEG and event review, with a separate selectable baseline interval.
H10 overview reports mean/min/max device HR, RR acceptance, RMSSD, SDNN, pNN50 and lnRMSSD using fixed nonoverlapping 60s or 300s windows. Screening uses existing range/median rules; pairs never cross rejections, continuity changes or detected receipt gaps. Provisional window qualification requires >=30 accepted RR, >=20 pairs, >=80% acceptance/receipt span, RR-duration estimate 80–120% of the window, and a single processing continuity segment. This is an engineering filter, not validated clinical confidence or verified NN. Insights compare a qualified full baseline window with a separate later qualified window of equal duration, name exact intervals and describe numerical changes without assigning autonomic/relaxation/sleep states. Breathing, posture and movement are contextual limitations. No LF/HF sympathetic-balance score is added.
Saved EEG bands are reconstructed from raw saved samples in a background isolate using the existing detrended Hann periodogram, 2s or 4s buffers, once-per-second steps and provisional amplitude/step/flatline checks. Recording/device segments, receipt gaps/reversals and malformed batches reset buffers. Motion-only batches do not interrupt EEG continuity. Supported recorded EEG rates are powers of two from 64–512 Hz. Bands use shared zero/scale, absolute µV² or relative 1–30 Hz percentage, channel selection, scale lock, baseline references and descriptive baseline/final-window values. Changing usable-channel availability and residual eye/muscle artifacts can affect comparisons. Very long band displays are sampled with an explicit warning; every source sample remains in exports.
Raw EEG/ECG replay loads bounded 10-second excerpts from originals rather than holding the entire waveform recording in memory. ECG decodes original type-0 signed PMD frames and preserves within-stream device intervals with a host-receipt anchor. EEG replay uses batch receipt/rate estimates; full BrainFlow timestamps remain in originals. Cross-device fine timing is not synchronized. Plots have axes, units and min/max; purple event lines and event listings give marker/practice context. ECG peak detection, ECG-derived HRV, configurable ECG filtering, automated state classification and statistical causal event analysis remain future work.

## Limits

Band feedback is exploratory and quality gates are provisional. Live band history is bounded; full unfiltered recordings are exported. Muse raw samples and summary counts are available in History/export; synchronized saved-session EEG charts and validated sleep/relaxation models remain future work. Participants currently use saved-name snapshots; cross-device stable profile IDs and multi-user assignment editing remain future work. Phone stimulation is a manual pacer, not an adaptive controller or an external exciter driver.
